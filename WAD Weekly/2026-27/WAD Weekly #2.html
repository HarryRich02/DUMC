🧗 LAST WEEK IN THE DUMC

NUBS Round 1
A group of keen comp kids visited Leeds last Wednesday for NUBS round 1. Good scores all round - as a team Durham came 8th so well done everyone! Special shout out to Chiara for an amazing performance to come 9th.
- Alasdair
Roped-up Social
Last Wednesday we engaged in our annual roped-up social to welcome new members into the club. A good time was had by all, despite some entanglements in queues at certain college bars. We had great turn out, so thank you to everyone who made it!
Freshers Meet
Last Sunday saw 5 cars full of keen climbers head up to Back Bowden. Both trad and bouldering were done aplenty, with lots of people trying both. Congratulations are due to Ethan for his first 7b boulder. Thanks to Jim for organising.
- Alasdair
Taster Sessions and the first Beer & Boulder of Term
We wrapped up the last of our taster sessions last Sunday after incredible numbers of new climbers! I was so pleased to see many first-time climbers and many returning to the sport. A special thank you to our Boulder Captain Ethan for organising them!
    Returning to our usual routine, we also saw one of the strongest Beer & Boulder turnouts (at least of my time) on Monday. As a reminder, we return to our usual routine of Beer & Boulder Mondays 7:30pm, and Team Training on Thursdays 8:00pm, both at the DCC and no need to book! I hope many of you stick around, and if you're interested in becoming a member look for the sign-up details on any of our chats, Instagram, or Facebook.
