Preseason
A great start to the season with nice and chill couple of sessions, having a bit of coaching towards the end. Thank to everyone that attended and a lesser thank you to those who told me to wear shorts on my first time outside (Leakey).
- Ethan
The gang reconvened a week (or two for some) early for some preseason adventures. The week started with a bright and early 4pm start at Eden Rock for some training and mock trials. After a catch-up with the ever delightful Martha, a great session ensued culminating in a trip to the Swan on return to Durham. Day 2 saw a trip to Back Bowden, as featured in the wonderful edit up on our Instagram. Trad and boulders were climbed, including President's second ever trad lead and first HVD.
    Wednesday and Thursday consisted of Team Training at the DCC and a visit to Newcastle Climbing Centre for some lead. Of course, the most important event of the week was the bar-crawl on Thursday night. This led to a few hangovers the next day during exec training, with various cures attempted ranging from drowning their sorrows in milk to the more sensible hair of the dog method.
    A number of members competed at Eden Rock's Beast of Eden comp to great success. Very good performance from everyone attending the first competition of the year with lots of people coming in the top of the bunch! Finally to round out the week, a few brave souls travelled 2hrs down the country to find some dry rock, and were successful in their quest! Sport and a touch of shallow-water soloing completed a fantastic week!
